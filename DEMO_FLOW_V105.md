# Student Hub v1.0.5 — recommended demo flow

Aim for a short story instead of showing every button.

## 1. Start with Today — 30–45 sec
Show that Student Hub answers two questions immediately:
- **What should I do next?**
- **Is my plan still realistic?**

Open the Action Center briefly to show that warnings are explainable and actionable, not just red badges.

## 2. Import a syllabus — 60 sec
Courses → Import syllabus:
1. upload the sample PDF;
2. Analyze with AI;
3. show the editable review screen;
4. explain that nothing is imported until the student confirms it.

This demonstrates trusted AI rather than opaque automation.

## 3. Planner — 60 sec
Open Planner:
- show Timetable versus My plan;
- show a deadline that needs coverage;
- Preview smart plan;
- point out that fixed classes are protected and proposed study sessions are reviewed before saving.

## 4. Study — 45 sec
Open a planned task:
- task/deadline context is already selected;
- contextual materials are recommended with reasons;
- timer length can be changed;
- full-screen focus exists;
- Finish & save records real progress so future plans can adapt.

## 5. Close the loop — 30 sec
Return to Today or Action Center:
- plan/risk state updates from the same academic data;
- Inbox, review, planner and reliability warnings point to concrete actions.

## One-sentence product story
**Student Hub turns scattered academic information into an explainable plan, keeps the plan realistic as work changes, and brings the right material into the exact study session where it is needed.**
