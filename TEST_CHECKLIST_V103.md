# Student Hub v1.0.3 test checklist

1. After GitHub Pages deploy, hard refresh once (`Ctrl + Shift + R`).
2. Study > choose a **planner task**:
   - run the timer for at least a few seconds;
   - press `Finish & save`;
   - confirm the new check-in appears with session time, target, before/after remaining work.
3. Choose **Made progress**:
   - edit `Count as progress` if desired;
   - save;
   - confirm Study history gets the session;
   - confirm the task/deadline remaining time decreases on Planner/Today;
   - confirm excess future auto-generated blocks are shortened/removed when necessary.
4. Repeat with **Needs more time**:
   - count some session minutes;
   - add extra estimate minutes;
   - save;
   - confirm remaining effort reflects `old - progress + extra`.
5. Repeat with **Finished**:
   - confirm the selected task becomes complete;
   - confirm future linked study blocks disappear;
   - confirm the deadline remaining value updates.
6. Study > choose a **deadline** rather than a task:
   - save progress;
   - confirm progress is applied across its open planner tasks and the deadline total stays consistent.
7. Study > **Free focus · timer only**:
   - finish a session;
   - confirm it saves directly without the planning check-in.
8. Confirm full-screen focus, duration presets, +/-5, materials, Study history and Library links still work.
9. Planner regression:
   - week arrows, course/type filters, Timetable/Deadlines/My plan lenses, density, drag/drop and Smart Plan preview still work.
10. Reload/sign in again and confirm completed work blocks do not reappear.
